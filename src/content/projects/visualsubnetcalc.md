A tool for quickly designing networks and collaborating on that design with others. It
focuses on expediting the work of network administrators, not academic subnetting math.
Put in a range, then visually split and join subnets within it. There is no database and
no back-end; everything runs in the browser and generates links and exports so users can
share their own designs.

I created this as a more feature-rich and modern version of a tool I found years ago and
absolutely love by davidc. I always used screenshot tools to add notes and colors, and
wanted a better way.
